# How many kilometers have you walked in Azeroth?

**Andarilho** tracks the distance your character travels in WoW Forever and converts yards to **meters and kilometers**.

## Features

- Total distance **on foot** in km, yards and meters, always on screen
- Separate totals for **on foot, mounted and swimming**
- Totals **per character** and across **all your characters**
- Distance for **today** and the **current session**
- **Daily report**: 7 days, 30 days or all time, this character or all, search by date or weekday, daily average and best day
- Small movable window that **minimizes to a minimap icon**
- Ignores **flight paths, teleports, boats, zeppelins** and running into walls
- English and Brazilian Portuguese, following your game client or your choice

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/andarilho` | Show or hide the window |
| `/andarilho report` | Open the daily report |
| `/andarilho total` | Print the totals in chat |
| `/andarilho language auto\|pt\|en` | Choose the language |
| `/andarilho minimap` | Show or hide the minimap icon |
| `/andarilho lock` | Lock or unlock the window |
| `/andarilho center` | Move the window back to the center |
| `/andarilho reset confirm` | Clear this character's count |

## Good to know

- Counting starts when you install the addon, because the game does not keep this history. The daily history starts with version 1.1.0.
- Inside dungeons the game hides your position, so distance is estimated from base speed while you move.
- Distance is horizontal, so climbing or falling does not add to it.

## Português

**Andarilho** conta a distância que o seu personagem percorre no WoW Forever e converte de yards para **metros e km**, separando a pé, montado e nadando, por personagem e somando todos. Taxi, teleporte, barco e correr contra a parede não contam.

Também tem relatório por dia com pesquisa, ícone no minimapa e escolha de idioma.

Comandos: `/andarilho`, `/andarilho relatorio`, `/andarilho total`, `/andarilho idioma auto|pt|en`, `/andarilho minimapa`, `/andarilho trancar`, `/andarilho centro`, `/andarilho reset confirmar`.
